“We have 42 employees. A California employee has worked 18 months and 1,400 hours, then requests leave for a serious health condition.”

A

“Eligible for 12 weeks under federal FMLA.”

employerEmployees = 42
employeesWithin75Miles = 42
tenureMonths = 18
hoursLast12M = 1,400
leaveReason = SERIOUS_HEALTH
CLAIM
FMLA_eligible = true
leaveWeeks = 12
② VALIDATE

SMT solver checks controlling rules

FEDERAL FMLA
employeesWithin75Miles ≥ 50
tenureMonths ≥ 12
hoursLast12M ≥ 1,250
CALIFORNIA CFRA
employerEmployees ≥ 5
tenureMonths ≥ 12
hoursLast12M ≥ 1,250
